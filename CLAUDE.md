# BuckleUp Quiz — notes for AI assistants

- Read `docs/PROGRESS.md` (plain-language status, simplification list) and `docs/STAGES.md` for what's done and what's next, and `docs/adr/` before changing structure.
- `Packages/QuizCore` must stay platform-free (Foundation only). It's tested on Linux:
  `make test-core-docker`.
- The iOS app can only be compiled on macOS. CI (`macos-26`) is the compile check from Linux.
- The `.xcodeproj` is generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`. Never commit it.
- No third-party runtime SDKs, analytics or tracking (ADR-0004, Kids Category). The only network
  calls are anonymous error reports to our own endpoint (ADR-0020): never names, answers or ids;
  and plain GETs of signed question updates from our CDN (ADR-0031), which send nothing.
- Content changes: `make validate`. Values and safety wording rules are in `docs/CONTENT.md`.
  Merged content reaches phones without an app update via the "Publish questions" workflow
  (ADR-0031). Question ids are permanent: never reuse or renumber one.
- New structural decision → new ADR. Finished a stage item → tick it in `docs/STAGES.md`.
