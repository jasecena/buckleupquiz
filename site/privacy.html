Privacy Policy

Draft — to be finalised before the app is released.

The short version: BuckleUp Quiz does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The app sends anonymous error reports so we can fix problems, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Microphone and speech

With the Family Pass, the app can listen for your child's spoken answers. Speech is turned into text on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere, and the app only listens straight after asking a question.

Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

Settings, player names, ages, scores and quiz progress are saved on your device. So that progress isn't lost if you reinstall the app or get a new iPhone, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account and in your iPhone's Keychain. These are Apple services that belong to you. We can't see this information, and it is never sent to us.

Anonymous error reports

When something goes wrong, for example the microphone won't start, the app notes what happened and sends it to our server so we can fix it. Each report holds the app version, the iPhone model and iOS version, what went wrong (such as an error message), and simple counts for each game (how many questions, how many answers were heard). Crash reports from Apple's MetricKit are included too.

Reports never include names, ages, answers, anything your child said, recordings, your location, your IP address or any identifier for you or your device. They are kept for 90 days. You can see exactly what's in them, or turn them off, in Settings → Privacy.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchases and links are protected by a parental gate.

Children's privacy

BuckleUp Quiz is designed for children and follows Apple's Kids Category rules. We do not collect personal information from children or adults. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous error reports described above.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, optional iCloud sync), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before releasing the change.
